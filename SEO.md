# Budgeto — SEO playbook

Goal: rank at the top of Google (US, English) for what Budgeto's customers search, and stay there.
Written Nov 10, 2026. Pages live at https://budgetoo-nine.vercel.app. Source of truth for titles/descriptions: `lib/seo/site.ts` (keep this file and that one in sync).

> How this was researched: web searches in this session (Nov 2026). The sandbox could not open pages directly, so sources were read through search-engine summaries of the pages listed. No keyword-volume tool (Ahrefs/Semrush/Search Console) was available, so **demand and competition below are estimates from the shape of the results page**, not measured volumes. Replace them with Search Console data once the site has 4–6 weeks of impressions.

## 1. The 10 rules (what Google and the best SEO teachers agree on for a new site with little authority)

1. **Write for people, from first-hand use.** Original information, real examples, depth a reader can act on. Content made mainly to attract search traffic, mass-produced, or written outside your expertise is flagged by Google's own self-assessment.
   Sources: Google, [Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) · Backlinko, [How Brian Dean would launch Backlinko today / SEO tutorial 2026](https://backlinko.com/seo-tutorial) (real data and experiments get cited) · Ahrefs, [SaaS SEO](https://ahrefs.com/blog/saas-seo/) (product-led content).
   → Budgeto: every page shows the **real output of the scan engine** on the bundled example statement (`lib/seo/sample-report.json`, regenerated by `scripts/sample-report.mts`).

2. **Start at the bottom of the funnel, with long-tail searches you can actually win.** Comparisons, "alternative", "<job> app", "how to <job>" — people close to acting. Prefer low keyword difficulty (Semrush 0–29) and check who ranks; a new domain can't beat a SERP of big brands on a head term.
   Sources: Backlinko, [SEO keywords](https://backlinko.com/hub/seo/seo-keywords) and [SEO strategy](https://backlinko.com/seo-strategy) (long-tail first, KD ≤ 29, 10–15 pieces on low-competition terms) · Ahrefs, [SaaS SEO](https://ahrefs.com/blog/saas-seo/) (BOFU comparison pages) · Edward Sturm, The Edward Show: [How I find low-competition keywords that actually convert](https://pocketcasts.com/podcast/the-edward-show/4a3893e0-2ef4-013c-f681-0acc26574db2/how-i-find-low-competition-keywords-that-actually-convert/50b4a861-1524-440c-bfeb-dc24004e6e0b) (BOFU still open; KD scores can mislead) · Search Engine Land, [BOFU keywords](https://searchengineland.com/guide/bofu-keywords).

3. **One page per search intent; read the top results before writing.** Match the format Google already rewards (guide vs tool vs comparison), then cover what those pages miss. Don't make two pages for one intent (they compete and split signals).
   Sources: Backlinko, [Keyword mapping](https://backlinko.com/keyword-mapping) · Google, [Consolidate duplicate URLs](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls).

4. **Put the answer first, then structure it.** A direct answer in the first lines, clear H2 sections, numbered steps, comparison tables. This is also what Google's AI features extract and cite — and Google says optimizing for them "is still SEO".
   Sources: Google, [2026 SEO in AI search guide for marketers](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/2026_seo_in_ai_search_guide_for_marketers.pdf) (summarized by [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-ai-search-optimization-guide/) and [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/googles-new-guide-for-ai-search-what-seo-really-needs-now/)) · Google, [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).

5. **A unique, descriptive title and meta description on every page, with its main search in it.** No boilerplate titles; one H1 that says what the page is.
   Sources: Google, [Influencing title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) · Google, [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) (snippets, descriptive URLs).

6. **Clean crawling and indexing.** Readable URLs, a self-referencing canonical, an XML sitemap of the public pages only, `noindex` on private screens (not a robots.txt block, which hides the noindex), real 404s.
   Sources: Google, [URL structure](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/url-structure) · Google, [rel=canonical](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls) · Google, [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).

7. **Link your pages to each other like a topic cluster.** A hub (Guides), spokes (each guide/comparison), descriptive anchor text, links to the canonical URL. Topical depth compensates for low domain authority early on.
   Sources: Google, [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide) (links) · Backlinko, [Pillar pages](https://backlinko.com/pillar-pages) · Ahrefs, [Mid-funnel content](https://ahrefs.com/blog/mid-funnel-content/).

8. **Structured data only for what the reader can see, and don't count on FAQ stars.** Organization, SoftwareApplication, BreadcrumbList, Article, FAQPage are fine when they match visible content; since 2023 FAQ rich results show only for well-known government and health sites.
   Sources: Google, [General structured data guidelines](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies) · Google, [Changes to HowTo and FAQ rich results (2023)](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes).

9. **Fast and usable on a phone.** Page experience is part of "helpful"; a slow, JS-heavy first screen loses mobile users first.
   Sources: Google, [Understanding page experience](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/page-experience) · Google, [Creating helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) (page experience section).

10. **Show who, how and why — and earn trust, then links.** Bylines where expected, sources for every claim, honest comparisons (including what the other app does better), no fake authors, reviews or numbers. Links come from genuinely useful assets (data, tools) and real mentions, not schemes.
    Sources: Google, [Creating helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) ("Who, How, and Why"; fake-author warning) · Ahrefs, [SaaS SEO](https://ahrefs.com/blog/saas-seo/) (honest comparison pages, free tools as link assets) · Nathan Gotch, [interview on Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/nathan-gotch-seo-entrepreneur/) (start from commercial keywords; improve what already ranks in Search Console).

What we deliberately skip: separate "GEO/AEO hacks" (Google's 2026 guide: it is the same SEO), mass AI pages, buying links, chasing Domain Rating (a third-party metric, not a Google ranking factor).

### What the top 3 cover, and what they miss

1. **find forgotten subscriptions** — Chime ("How to save by canceling subscriptions you forgot about"), Clark.com ("Find and cancel unused subscriptions"), Tiller / subtracker.io. *Cover:* check statements, email, app stores, quarterly review. *Miss:* what statement codes mean, yearly renewals, a way to do it in seconds. → home is the tool; the how-to is page 7.
2. **bank statement analyzer** — Ledger AI (alternativeto), DocuClipper, StatementConvert.ai / Overchat. *Cover:* PDF→CSV conversion, categories. *Miss:* a subscription/fee focus, an example output, plain privacy terms, what it can't do.
3. **rocket money alternative** — alternativeto.net, robberger.com ("5 Rocket Money alternatives"), subtracker.io. *Cover:* lists (Monarch, YNAB, PocketGuard), "no bank link" apps with manual entry. *Miss:* automatic detection without a bank link; what Rocket Money still does better (cancellation service, bill negotiation).
4. **ynab alternative / vs** — robberger.com/ynab, thecfoclub.com, pocketsmith.com; for "rocket money vs ynab": rocketmoney.com, useorigin.com, fool.com. *Cover:* budgeting-app lists, prices. *Miss:* the "I only want to find leaks" job; setup time; using both.
5. **copilot money alternative** — openalternative.co, beancount.io, subtracker.io. *Cover:* Monarch, YNAB, Actual. *Miss:* outdated device facts (Copilot has had a web app since Dec 2025; still no Android app), the no-link option.
6. **college students** — bestmoney.com, joingerald.com (many thin pages), mybanktracker.com. *Cover:* YNAB student offer, Goodbudget, PocketGuard. *Miss:* student-specific leaks (trials, lapsed student pricing, doubles, the gym back home), no-login option.
7. **how to find subscriptions** — Chime, Clark, Tiller, Vice (Apple). *Cover:* statements, email, app stores. *Miss:* exact menu paths (PayPal, Amazon, Play Store), card-updater trap, decoding codes, a 10-minute routine.
8. **stop recurring charges** — CFPB "How do I stop automatic payments", bank help pages, consumer blogs. *Cover:* revoke + stop payment. *Miss:* the full sequence incl. app-store billing, credit-card disputes (FTC 60-day rule), 2026 status of click-to-cancel.
9. **bank fees waived** — 19pine.ai, iwillteachyoutoberich.com (Ramit Sethi), Yahoo Finance. *Cover:* call and ask, direct-deposit waivers. *Miss:* finding every fee first, fee-by-fee prevention, an escalation path.
11. **tracker without linking bank** — subtracker.io, App Store apps (CustomSubs, SubTrackr), recurdash.com. *Miss:* every one is manual entry; nobody detects subscriptions automatically without a bank link — Budgeto does, from the PDF.
12. **APPLE.COM/BILL** — discussions.apple.com threads, Google Sites spam pages, joingerald.com. *Miss:* one clear page with all Apple descriptors, how to split the line into subscriptions, refunds, and when it's fraud.

## 3. Technical (done, Nov 10 2026)

- Every public page: one title ≤ 60, one description ≤ 155, one H1, self canonical, OG image (`/og/<slug>`), `lib/seo/site.ts` registry.
- `app/sitemap.ts` (public pages only) · `app/robots.ts` (blocks `/api/` only) · private screens `noindex` via `proxy.ts` `X-Robots-Tag` + meta (app, auth, `/s/…` shared reports).
- JSON-LD (`lib/seo/jsonld.tsx`): Organization, WebSite (home), SoftwareApplication (prices **read live from Kwop** where the page shows them; elsewhere the free scan only), FAQPage (all answers in the HTML), BreadcrumbList, Article (guides).
- Speed: hero no longer waits for JavaScript; scroll reveals are pure CSS; decorative animations load near the screen; Supabase client loads on sign-up click; Kwop script `lazyOnload`.
- Real 404 with links; footer links every SEO page (topic cluster).
- Check it any time: `node scripts/seo-check.mjs https://budgetoo-nine.vercel.app`.
