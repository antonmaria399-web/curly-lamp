# Budgeto — VSL voice-over script (~58 s)

The landing page plays a motion-design VSL (`components/landing/vsl.tsx`, 8 scenes, muted).
Record this voice-over over a screen capture of it (or your own video) and save it as `public/vsl.mp4`:
the page then shows your video instead of the motion version, automatically.

| # | Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:06 | A checking balance falling fast | "Payday hits. Two weeks later… it's gone. Where did it go?" |
| 2 | 0:06–0:13 | A bank statement scrolling, full of codes | "You open your statement and it's six pages of codes nobody can read." |
| 3 | 0:13–0:21 | Subscription lines light up | "And hiding in there: subscriptions you forgot. A gym. A storage unit. Three streaming apps." |
| 4 | 0:21–0:28 | The PDF drops into Budgeto | "So I dropped my statement PDF into Budgeto. No bank login. Just the PDF." |
| 5 | 0:28–0:36 | Steps tick: reading, recurring charges, fees | "Its AI read every single line in seconds." |
| 6 | 0:36–0:44 | Leak meter counts up (example: $211.86/mo) | "Two hundred eleven dollars a month. Leaking. Every month." |
| 7 | 0:44–0:52 | Savings counter (example: +$1,163/yr) | "I cancelled three I never use. That's over a thousand dollars a year back in my pocket." |
| 8 | 0:52–0:58 | "Find my leaks." | "Grab last month's statement. Find your leaks tonight. Budgeto." |

Short-video hook (TikTok / Reels): **"I found a $200 monthly subscription I forgot about in 5 seconds."**
Show scene 3 → 6 in 10 seconds, then the Faceless Export screens from your own report.
All amounts above are the bundled *sample* statement's real results; say "for example" or use your own numbers.
